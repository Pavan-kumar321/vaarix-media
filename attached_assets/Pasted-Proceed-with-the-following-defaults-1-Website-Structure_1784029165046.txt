Proceed with the following defaults:
1. Website Structure
- Build a premium single-page scrolling website.
- Every section should transition smoothly with cinematic scroll animations.
- The experience should feel luxurious and highly interactive rather than like a standard business website.
2. Lead Capture
- Build a fully functional lead capture form.
- Store every submission in the database.
- Fields:
  • Full Name
  • Business Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Website / Instagram (Optional)
  • Monthly Marketing Budget (Dropdown)
  • Services Interested In
  • Message
- Add client-side validation.
- Show a premium success animation after submission.
- Do not configure email notifications yet.
3. Media
- Use premium placeholder videos and images.
- Use realistic restaurant marketing examples, branding, posters, websites, food photography and social media creatives.
- Make every placeholder easily replaceable later.

5. Animations
Focus heavily on premium motion.
Include:
• Smooth page load animation
• Scroll-triggered fade and scale effects
• Blur-to-sharp reveals
• Floating elements
• Auto-scrolling portfolio rows
• Infinite logo marquee
• Autoplay muted videos
• Hover interactions
• Glassmorphism transitions
• Animated counters
• Magnetic buttons
• Smooth scrolling
• Subtle parallax
• Staggered text reveals
Animations should remain elegant, lightweight, and performant.
6. Performance
Optimize for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
Maintain 60 FPS where possible.
Lazy-load videos and images.
Avoid unnecessary heavy animations.
